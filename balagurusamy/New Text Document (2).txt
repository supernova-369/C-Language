Logical errors are mistakes in the program's algorithm or reasoning that cau
 it to produce incorrect or unexpected output, even though it runs without crashing. 
Compilers do not detect these errors because the code is syntactically valid;
they are typically found during program execution and debugging by the programmer.




Data errors are not a standard category of errors detected by
a compiler. Errors related to data, such as division by 
zero or accessing invalid memory, generally manifest as run-time errors 
(occurring during program execution) or are a result of logical errors or bad input data.
